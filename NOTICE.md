# Asset provenance

Wendy's layout generator is in `src/skin.py`. The initial standalone export
corresponds to the hardware-tested skin revision 2.5.3.

`src/Denon SCLIVE4 Screen.png` retains the sprite sheet from the stock
`Denon SCLIVE4 Screen` package supplied for this project.
`src/dialogs.xml` retains its power-off, USB-exit and playing-deck load-security
dialog definitions. Their existing names and behavior are preserved.

Denon DJ and VirtualDJ names identify the compatible hardware and software.
Wendy is a community project and is not an official Denon DJ or VirtualDJ product.
